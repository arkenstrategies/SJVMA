Skills Dashboard

Closing the Skills Gap

This dashboard brings employers, educators, and talent together.

SJVMA connects manufacturers, schools, and training providers across the Central Valley around one shared signal. Employers register their companies and share skills needs at every level, from senior leadership to the front line. Educators use that signal to build programs around real demand. Students train for the skills that lead to in-demand jobs.

For Employers
A direct line to the region's training providers, and wage benchmarking once your assessment is complete.
For Educators
Real, current employer demand by skill, not guesswork, to keep programs responsive.
For Students and Workers
Training pathways built around the skills that actually get people hired and promoted.
